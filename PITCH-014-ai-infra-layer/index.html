mechanism

The layer under your team's Claude — not another seat, not a new subscription.

Your team already pays for Claude. Governframe is a hosted MCP/tooling layer their own sessions connect to: shared memory, a curated tool roster per role, and one admin screen that shows who's doing what — without Governframe ever touching anyone's account or credentials.

who it's for

Small teams past the point of "everyone figures it out themselves."

Agencies, consultancies, and dev/ops shops of 3–15 people already running multiple people through paid Claude subscriptions for real client work — past the point where one person's ad hoc setup is enough, before the size where Anthropic's Enterprise sales motion makes sense. The buyer is the team lead who's already said, in effect: "I don't want everyone using it willy-nilly."

limits & faq

The five hardest questions, answered straight.

Isn't this just reselling Claude access?

No. BYO-subscription means your own Claude session/account connects to our hosted layer — we never touch, hold, or operate your credentials. Anthropic's own developer docs name exactly this pattern (third-party companies running remote MCP servers a user's own session connects to) as a documented, observed-in-the-wild shape — not a confirmed blessing in so many words, but a real precedent, not a guess.

Why pay for this when I can clone a free GitHub "agent roster" repo myself?

You can — and that proves the demand is real. But that's a config artifact you maintain yourself, forever, alone, with no shared memory layer, no governance/audit view, and no isolation design across a team. We sell the running, maintained, governed service — the free repo is the honest ceiling on what one technically confident person can DIY.

Is our data actually isolated from your other clients?

Honestly: logical per-tenant separation at this price tier, not dedicated infrastructure or a formal compliance certification. Composio and Glama — the closest named competitors — both reserve real (SOC2/VPC-level) isolation for their enterprise tiers too. If you need that level, we'll tell you this isn't the right fit yet rather than imply otherwise.

Why not just buy Claude Team or Enterprise instead?

Team ($20–125/seat/mo) buys governance controls — SSO, admin, billing — but no shared data/memory/orchestration layer. Enterprise adds compliance depth but is a sales-led, seat-minimum motion most 5–15 person teams won't clear and don't need yet. We sit in that gap: the layer on top of Team, without jumping tiers or building it yourself.

What's actually yours to sell here versus just repackaged Claude?

The hosted memory/KB layer (a real, independently priced category — see mem0's pricing), the curated/governed tool roster (Composio and Zapier MCP prove tool-calling access is independently sellable), and the admin isolation/audit view across a team (nobody bundles this with the other two at small-team pricing today). What's NOT ours to sell: the model itself — that's Claude's, through your own subscription.
