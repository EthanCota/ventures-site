What it is

Three modules, one existing relationship

Vornix already answers the phone. Cuspid is the next layer up: turning the clinical and administrative work that happens around that phone call — charting the visit, reminding the patient, keeping the practice-management system in sync — into the same trusted product, instead of three separate vendor relationships.

Separately, today: Ambient charting Recall & confirmation SMS PMS-assist → One Cuspid relationship

Who it's for

The existing Vornix book — not a new market

An existing or prospective Vornix client: a single-location or small-group (2-4 dentist) independent dental practice in San Diego County, owner-operated, already running or evaluating Vornix's voice-reception product.

  • Runs one of the 8 PMS platforms the Kolla Unify bridge covers: Dentrix, Dentrix Enterprise, Open Dental, Eaglesoft, Denticon, Dolphin, WinOMS, OrthoTrac. [sourced, RESEARCH.md §2.3]
  • Not a 10+-location DSO-adjacent group — that's a different buyer, enterprise procurement, outside Vornix's current motion.

FAQ

Questions before the roadmap review

Five hardest objections, answered from this idea's own research — reused from PITCH.md, not softened for this page.

Why pay extra when Freed/Heidi exist cheaper?

Neither is dental-charting-shaped; the wedge is fit, not price.

Does it actually write into our PMS?

Honest answer: unconfirmed today. Generates a note ready for clinician review, not a guaranteed direct chart write, until Kolla's field-level write completeness is tested per PMS.

Is it HIPAA-compliant?

Infrastructure BAA chain is largely in place; a PMS-specific BAA is a real per-practice item still to handle before write-access ships.

Does the Kolla fee raise my cost below 10 pooled locations?

Yes, structurally, until Vornix pools 10+ locations; Vornix absorbs or flat-fees this near-term.

Why extend instead of just raising the existing retainer price?

Cross-selling a trusted vendor is lower-CAC than either a price-risk-churn move or a cold net-new sales motion this idea has no built channel for.
