First, plainly—

What it is

A single curated introduction, made by a person who knows both sides — not a swipe deck, not a self-serve directory, not a membership you pay into and hope pays off.

Opt-in only

Consent-first, always

Every person in the pool joined on purpose. Nobody is scraped, imported, or "found" without asking first. A person we haven't gotten a yes from is a lead, never a match.

Human-made

A person reads both sides

No algorithm decides the match. A human who has actually talked to both people proposes the pairing and explains, in plain language, why it makes sense.

One-time, not a subscription

Pay for the intro, not a seat

$150, once, when you get your intro. No monthly fee, no annual dues, no membership tier to justify re-upping.

This is meant for—

Who it's for

Pre-seed and bootstrapped San Diego-area founders and solo operators — people below the revenue thresholds that gate the existing peer networks, who want one specific, well-vetted connection instead of a membership they can't yet justify.

Founders below the EO / YPO line

Entrepreneurs' Organization requires at least $1M in annual revenue to join; YPO requires $16M+ in revenue or 50+ full-time employees. [sourced, eonetwork.org/membership & ypo.org/membership, fetched 2026-07-29] Everyone below those lines is currently served by nothing built for curated, one-off intros.

Investors and advisors willing to meet one good founder

Not a pitch-deck inbox to manage — one specific, pre-vetted conversation at a time, sourced through a real co-working hub or builder circle, not a cold-outreach list.

Here is exactly how it goes—

How it works

Four steps, no software, no self-serve browsing.

  1. You tell us the one connection you actually need

    Not "investors in general" — the specific kind of person and conversation that would move things forward right now.

  2. We check our opt-in pool

    Sourced from co-working hubs, builder communities, and direct referral — never scraped, never cold-added.

  3. A human proposes the match and why

    Both sides get a short, honest reasoning note before anyone agrees to anything.

  4. Both sides say yes — then you meet

    No intro happens without both people opting in a second time, at the actual moment of connection.
