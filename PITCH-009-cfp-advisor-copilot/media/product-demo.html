What using TallyHelm actually looks like

Three scripted scenarios covering the four things Carsten's own hand-built dashboard already does for him: KPI targets, contact cadence, form autofill, and low-friction client data entry. All names, numbers, and client details on this page are invented for illustration and bounded against real comparable figures in RESEARCH.md — none of it is a real client or a real result.
