What it is

One page, your brand, calibrated to who's actually staying.

GuestWaypoint takes the property, the guest's travel-party type, and their spend level, and generates a curated day-plan and a targeted food/bar map — kid-fun options, live music, breweries and wine bars, or higher-end picks — scoped to what's actually walkable or driveable from where they're staying. It ships to the guest as your property's own guide, not a third-party app they have to discover and install.

Spend-calibrated, not generic

Recommendations shift with the guest's stated budget tier — a family stretching a long-weekend budget sees a different bar list than a couple on a splurge trip.

Anchored to the property

Every suggestion is scoped to real distance from the listing or hotel — the "easier than Google Maps for what's actually around here" version, not a citywide list.

Answers the questions before they're asked

The same handful of questions every guest sends — kid-friendly spot, good happy hour, date-night place — get answered up front, in the guide, before the message thread starts.

End user — who it's for

Couples & families-of-4 visiting San Diego

  • Weekend up to a week-plus trip, basic itinerary already booked
  • Filling in "the good parts": kid-fun activities, live music, craft breweries and wineries, higher-class options when the trip calls for it
  • Want it calibrated to their spend level and to where they're actually staying — not a citywide top-10 list

Who it's for

Buyer — who pays

San Diego STR hosts & small boutique hotels

  • Tier 3/4 STRO-licensed short-term rental operators — the professionalized, higher-volume tier of the city's own licensing program
  • Concentrated in family/couple-tourist-dense neighborhoods: Mission Beach, Pacific Beach, Gaslamp/Downtown, North Park, La Jolla
  • Hosting 2-7 night stays, already fielding repeat guest questions by text or email

What ships in the guide

Day-plan generator

Guest-party type + spend tier + length of stay → a scaffolded day-by-day plan, editable by the host before it goes live.

Targeted food/bar map

A short, walkable/driveable list — not a Google Maps dump — tagged by price tier, vibe, and family-friendliness, anchored to the property's address.

Host-branded page

The guide carries the property's name and look, not GuestWaypoint's — guests never see the vendor behind it.

Optional booking revenue-share

In-guide activity links can route through an affiliate booking layer; commission splits with the host, disclosed per FTC rules on every link (full citation trail retained in the provenance record).

One page to maintain

Hosts don't build content — the underlying data/venue engine maintains it; the host's only job is approving the property profile.

Portfolio add-on

Property managers running several STRO-licensed listings add each one at a reduced per-listing rate instead of a second full subscription.

Pricing
