What it is

A curated discovery layer, not another hosting tool.

San Diego's tech & AI scene runs through 150+ meetups, user groups, and community orgs (sdtechscene.org's own count) plus a scatter of Luma pages, Meetup groups, and Instagram accounts. Hackerlantern ingests all of it into one weekly digest and a live site — the front door the scene doesn't currently have.

Not a hosting tool. Luma and Meetup help an organizer publish their own event. Hackerlantern shows a resident everything happening across every organizer, gym-for-devs, and account at once.

Not a general city guide. Do512-class citywide guides cover concerts, food, and drink specials. Hackerlantern is vertical-specific: tech and AI only, at launch — the depth a general guide can't match.

Who it's for

Two sides, one weekly signal.

Meetup organizers, coworking venues, DevRel

A group organizer running 1-3 meetups, a coworking space hosting a monthly tech night, a bootcamp recruiting locally, or a dev-tool startup doing developer-relations reach — anyone who wants more attendees and has no single channel that reaches beyond their own list.

SD tech & AI residents

People who currently check Meetup, Luma, sdtechscene.org, and scattered social accounts by hand to find out what's on this week. The digest and site are free for this side — it's the audience the buyer side pays to reach.

Listings

What actually ships.

  • 01/W

    Weekly digest

    Every event, launch, and meetup in the next 7 days, one email/site update — CAN-SPAM compliant (physical address, one-click opt-out) from day one.

  • 02/D

    Live directory + calendar

    A standing, always-current view of every group, venue, and recurring event in the vertical — searchable by topic, date, and neighborhood.

  • 03/F

    Featured/sponsor slots

    An organizer's next 1-2 events pinned at the top of the digest and site, plus a standing directory listing.

  • 04/S

    Source-coverage dashboard (sponsor-facing)

    Shows a sponsor exactly which sources are being tracked and how fresh the data is — trust infrastructure for a discovery product, not just a pretty page. See the simulated walkthrough below.
