§3 · Who it's for

Built for the underutilizer, not the beginner

✓ THIS IS FOR YOU IF

  • You're already paying for Claude Pro ($20/mo) or Max (from $100/mo) — for at least a couple months.
  • You use Claude Code or Cowork sometimes, but there's no CLAUDE.md, no skills, no memory — just the default.
  • You're a solo consultant, agency owner, or small-team operator (1-10 people) — technical enough to run a CLI, not looking to architect an agent config yourself.
  • You've felt the "I keep re-explaining the same context" tax.

✕ NOT A FIT IF

  • You're an enterprise team with your own platform/DevOps staff — you'll build this in-house or want a hosted managed layer instead (different offer, see below).
  • You've never touched Claude Code — this isn't onboarding, it's tuning what you already run.
  • You want us running infrastructure on your behalf 24/7 — that's a hosted service, not this one.

§4 · Fence

What this is not

Loadout sells packaged configuration and a setup/upkeep service — files, skill definitions, memory scaffolding, and setup labor delivered to you and installed into your own Claude account, running on your own subscription. Nothing is hosted on our infrastructure. Nobody but you ever logs into your account — that's the bright line in Anthropic's own Consumer Terms, and Loadout is built to sit clearly on the compliant side of it. If what you actually want is someone else's servers running your agents around the clock, that's a different, adjacent product — not this one.

§7 · Limits & FAQ

What this doesn't do, and the hard questions

Docs-style, not sales-style — including the one where the honest answer used to be "I just downgraded instead."

Q1

Why would I pay you when GitHub has free templates with 140K stars?

The free templates are the raw materials, not the finished job — davila7/claude-code-templates and similar repos give you components; they don't know YOUR workflow, YOUR data, or which of the dozens of skills in awesome-claude-code (51,220★) actually apply to what you do. The install/curation/maintenance labor is the product, not the config files themselves.

[sourced — public GitHub star counts]
Q2

I already tried something like this and just downgraded my subscription instead — why wouldn't I do that again?

Fair, and that's the right move if the problem really is "I don't need this much capability." But if the honest answer is "I have capability I'm not using well," downgrading trades the symptom for a permanent ceiling — you'll hit the same wall on a cheaper plan sooner. This offer is priced to be cheaper than the gap between what you're already paying for Max ($100+/mo) and what you'd get from a cheaper tier — worth testing which one is actually true before downgrading.

[named pattern from a real public forum post, not a hypothetical]
Q3

Is this even allowed under Anthropic's terms?

Yes with an important boundary, stated plainly: nobody but you ever logs into your account. Anthropic's Consumer Terms bar account/credential sharing and reselling access to their Services — this offer does neither; it delivers configuration artifacts and setup labor, installed by you, into your own account.

"You may not share your Account login information, Anthropic API key, or Account credentials with anyone else. You also may not make your Account available to anyone else." — Anthropic Consumer Terms §2, quoted directly
[our working legal read, not a legal guarantee — see risk notes in the internal appendix]
Q4

How do I know the setup fee isn't just marked-up dev time?

It's priced against agent-orchestration labor, not a $100-200/hr traditional dev-shop rate — that's the actual reason the fee can sit below typical agency benchmarks and still carry margin.

[derived — not a vendor quote]
Q5

What if my needs change and this stops fitting in 3 months?

The retainer includes a periodic review specifically because agent config drifts as your work changes — Growth and Pro tiers both include a recurring check-in, not a one-and-done handoff. If your needs grow into wanting managed, always-on hosted infrastructure rather than a config you run yourself, that's a different, adjacent offer (PITCH-014/C16) — this stays a config+service product by design, not a bait-and-switch into hosting.
