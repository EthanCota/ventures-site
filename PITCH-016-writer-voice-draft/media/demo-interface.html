Step 2 — Generate a draft, and judge it yourself

The writer gives Quillecho a topic: "a piece about why I'm cutting back on social media posting." No automated "voice-match score" is shown — just the archive next to the draft, for the writer to judge.

From the archive (excerpt)

"I used to think posting more was the job. Turns out the job was writing something worth reading, and posting was just the delivery truck. I noticed this the week I missed three days on purpose and nothing broke — the newsletter still landed, the inbox still filled. So here's the question I keep circling back to: what am I actually optimizing for?"

Draft, from Quillecho

"I used to think showing up online every day was the job. It isn't — the job is writing something worth someone's five minutes, and posting is just the delivery truck. I found this out by accident, cutting back for a week and watching nothing break. Which leaves an obvious question I can't fully answer yet: what was I actually optimizing for?"

Both blocks above are invented for this demo — no real writer's archive or output. The point being illustrated: matched rhythm and phrasing, not a copy-paste.
